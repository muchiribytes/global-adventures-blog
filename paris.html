Must-See Urban Landmarks

Our absolute highlight was watching the Eiffel Tower burst into its sparkling light show right at dusk. Sitting on the Champ de Mars with a blanket, it felt like the entire city paused to appreciate the view.

Culinary Delights

We spent our mornings tracking down small neighborhood boulangeries. There is nothing quite like a perfectly flaky, warm croissant paired with a rich espresso at a sidewalk table while watching the city wake up.

Personal Reflections: Twilight by the Seine

Our favorite memory wasn't a crowded museum, but rather a late-night stroll along the riverbanks near Notre-Dame. A group of local acoustic musicians had gathered underneath one of the stone bridges, and we sat on the steps listening to them play.
